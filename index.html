More Than Just Coffee.

We started with a simple idea: coffee tastes better when someone cares about every step. We buy small-lot beans from growers we trust, roast them in small batches, and brew each cup with patience.

Our baristas know your order by the second visit. Our tables are for lingering. Come for the espresso, stay for the company.
